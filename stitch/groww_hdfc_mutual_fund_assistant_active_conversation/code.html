Investor Query 11:42 AM

What is the exit load structure for HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund Direct Growth, and does it apply to SIP redemptions?

smart_toy
Groww Facts Engine Factsheet Verified (October 2026)

For redemptions within 1 year (365 days) from the date of allotment, an exit load of 1.00% is levied on the redeemed units. No exit load is charged for units redeemed after completing 365 days from allotment.

Application to SIPs: The 1-year holding period is calculated separately for each individual monthly SIP installment from its respective date of allotment (FIFO — First In First Out protocol).
